What it does

liverics detects the song playing and shows its lyrics line-by-line, advancing automatically in time with the music — karaoke-style subtitles for whatever's on. The lyric renderer is one reusable core; different "detectors" feed it the current song and playback position.

Install

It's an unpacked Chromium extension (Chrome / Edge / Brave / Arc):

  1. Go to chrome://extensions
  2. Turn on Developer mode (top-right)
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the extension/ folder
  4. Open music.youtube.com and play a song

The overlay appears near the bottom and follows along. Hit × to hide it.

Live lyrics from LRCLIB · best-effort title + duration matching · Chromium only for now.
